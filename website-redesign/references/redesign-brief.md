# REDESIGN-BRIEF.md Template

Write to `.website-info-collector/REDESIGN-BRIEF.md`. Short, evidence-based, no approval needed.
Facts and constraints only — no visual direction (that is `DIRECTION.md`) and no copied page
content (that stays in `pages/*.md`).

```markdown
# Redesign Brief — <Brand> (<original URL>)

## 1. Brand
What they do · audience · value proposition · personality & tone (quote copy) · identity to keep
(logo, core hue, distinctive fonts) · real trust signals · primary / secondary conversion

## 2. Current problems (with evidence: page, errors.md)
UX · visual · technical (speed, SEO gaps, a11y, errors)

## 3. Routes
| Original route | New route | Template type | Page file | Notes (redirect? skipped why?) |

## 4. Forms & integrations
| Form or integration | Pages | Endpoint / provider | Keep as-is / UI-only with honest state |

## 5. Must preserve
Routes, content, forms, integrations, SEO metadata, JSON-LD, verbatim legal text.

## 6. Constraints & assumptions
Framework, hosting hints, locales, anything assumed.

## 7. Iterations
(one line per feedback round: feedback, what changed, routes touched)
```
